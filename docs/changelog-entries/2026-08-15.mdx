description: "Meet Zynbox"
---

Version 5.2 introduces Zynbox, our new name and identity across the app. Everything you already use continues to work, now with consistent Zynbox branding and links to [zynbox.cloud](https://zynbox.cloud).

- The assistant, onboarding, settings, digests, meeting briefs, and in-app messages now use the Zynbox name.
- Transactional emails, invitations, summaries, reconnection notices, booking messages, and email footers now use Zynbox branding and links.
- Slack, Telegram, and connected-chat experiences now show Zynbox in welcome messages, notifications, commands, and help text.
- New referral signatures say “Drafted by Zynbox,” while existing “Drafted by Inbox Zero” signatures continue to work correctly.
- The CLI and setup guides now present Zynbox while preserving existing commands and compatibility with the working hosted API.
